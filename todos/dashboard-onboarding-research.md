# Post-signup tutor onboarding and profile setup research

**Access date for every source:** 2026-10-10  
**Scope:** First-party tutoring-platform help/onboarding material and first-party UX/design-system guidance only. “Verified fact” means the cited source explicitly states or demonstrates the point. “Recommendation” is an interpretation for FindMyTutor, not a claim that the source mandates the design.

## Executive summary

The clearest comparable-platform evidence is from italki. Its teacher onboarding separates application, review, identity verification, credentials, profile media, teaching languages, and ongoing profile updates. It also treats availability as a first-class setting rather than a substitute for profile media. Official UX guidance supports a resumable, task-oriented flow: ask focused questions, preserve entered values after errors, validate on continuation/submission, make upload requirements explicit, and provide a summary with change links. For FindMyTutor, use a dashboard checklist with saved progress, a public-profile preview, and separate “publish-ready” requirements from optional optimization.

## Verified facts from tutoring platforms

### italki: onboarding is staged and includes a post-approval setup window

- Applicants submit an application, receive a decision, and—when pre-approved—must complete onboarding within 15 calendar days. After onboarding and account setup, they are ready to teach. **Verified fact.** Source: [“How do I apply to become a teacher?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/213716298-How-do-I-apply-to-become-a-teacher), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- Applicants choose one teacher type: Professional Teacher or Community Tutor. The platform explains the distinction before application, which is a useful example of asking for a consequential role choice early. **Verified fact.** Source: [“How do I apply to become a teacher?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/213716298-How-do-I-apply-to-become-a-teacher) and [“Difference between Professional Teacher and Community Tutor”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002978766-Difference-between-Professional-Teacher-and-Community-Tutor), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- A teacher can update the profile after becoming a teacher, including an introduction video and another teaching language. **Verified fact.** Source: [“How do I apply to become a teacher?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/213716298-How-do-I-apply-to-become-a-teacher), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Photo and display identity

- italki says the profile picture creates a student’s first impression and recommends a friendly, professional-looking, clear, well-lit, close-up, recognizable image. It rejects blurry images, busy backgrounds, other people, sunglasses, extreme distance, excessive brightness/darkness, logos/contact information, misleading edits, AI-generated images, and virtual images. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Profile Display Name and Picture Guidelines”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206351988-Profile-Display-Name-and-Picture-Guidelines), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- The same page specifies JPG/GIF/PNG, a 2 MB maximum, and an ideal minimum of 400 × 400 pixels. Photo changes can take up to five business days to review. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Profile Display Name and Picture Guidelines”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206351988-Profile-Display-Name-and-Picture-Guidelines), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- italki explicitly directs unavailable teachers to availability settings instead of removing or disguising the profile picture. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Profile Display Name and Picture Guidelines”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206351988-Profile-Display-Name-and-Picture-Guidelines), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Bio, subjects/languages, experience, and credentials

- italki distinguishes Professional Teachers, who have educator training and/or extensive professional teaching experience, from Community Tutors, who provide conversation practice. Professional Teachers can provide resources, prepared materials, and structured lesson plans. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Difference between Professional Teacher and Community Tutor”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002978766-Difference-between-Professional-Teacher-and-Community-Tutor), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- Professional Teachers must demonstrate qualifications for every language they intend to teach. Accepted evidence includes an accredited language-teaching certificate, a relevant university degree, a teaching license, or proof of employment at a language-teaching institution. **Verified fact.** Source: [“How do I become a Professional Teacher? What are the requirements for certificates?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206352058-How-do-I-become-a-Professional-Teacher-What-are-the-requirements-for-certificates), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- italki says original credential documents are not published; students see the description rather than the original documents. **Verified fact.** Source: [“How do I become a Professional Teacher? What are the requirements for certificates?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206352058-How-do-I-become-a-Professional-Teacher-What-are-the-requirements-for-certificates), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- The teacher profile exposes languages taught and spoken, and adding another teaching language requires language selection, a video in the languages being taught, and—for Professional Teachers—supporting credentials. **Verified fact.** Source: [“How to add another teaching language?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900007656563-How-to-add-another-teaching-language), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- italki’s current profile-optimization feature gives teachers profile views, video plays, booking starts, new bookings, student demographics, and benchmarks; it also offers optional AI suggestions for the “About Me” section that the teacher can edit, accept, or discard. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Boost Your Profile Feature for italki Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/46097267780249-Boost-Your-Profile-Feature-for-italki-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Introduction video and proof of teaching quality

- italki requires the teacher to appear in the introduction video, limits it to 1–3 minutes, and requires speaking all languages the teacher wants to teach. It prohibits personal contact information, promotion of other services, and unlicensed copyrighted media. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Introduction video guidelines and requirements”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206352028-Introduction-video-guidelines-and-requirements), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- The guidance asks teachers to introduce who they are, languages, credentials/work experience, lesson approach, activities/materials, and personality. It recommends a stable horizontal camera, eye-level framing, front lighting, clear sound, and a clean, non-distracting background. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Introduction video guidelines and requirements”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206352028-Introduction-video-guidelines-and-requirements), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Availability and teaching readiness

- italki booking shows the learner a teacher’s available date/time in the learner’s timezone, after selecting language, lesson type, and duration. **Verified fact.** Source: [“How to schedule a lesson on italki”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900007728643-How-to-schedule-a-lesson-on-italki), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- italki requires a stable internet connection, a microphone, and a webcam; headphones are recommended. **Verified fact.** Source: [“What are the technology requirements for teachers?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002978586-What-are-the-technology-requirements-for-teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Verification and trust

- italki runs online identity verification for all new teacher applicants, asks for a passport or government-issued identification, and describes the process as intended to reduce fraudulent accounts and protect teachers and students. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Identity Verification Guidelines for Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002630763-Identity-Verification-Guidelines-for-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.
- italki states that the verification process is handled by Sumsub and that italki does not store personal information during the process; this is a platform-specific implementation, not a universal requirement. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Identity Verification Guidelines for Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002630763-Identity-Verification-Guidelines-for-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Other first-party tutoring-platform signals

- Tutor.com describes tutoring as remote and flexible, with tutors logging on when available. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Become an online tutor with Tutor.com”](https://www.tutor.com/apply), Tutor.com, accessed 2026-10-10.
- Tutor.com’s public application page identifies tutors as teachers, professors, adjuncts, PhD students, and industry professionals. This supports collecting experience/qualification context, but it does not document a specific post-signup profile-completion UI. **Verified fact and source limitation.** Source: [“Become an online tutor with Tutor.com”](https://www.tutor.com/apply), Tutor.com, accessed 2026-10-10.

## Verified facts from official UX and design-system guidance

### Break onboarding into focused questions

- GOV.UK’s question-page pattern recommends question pages for services and shows a focused question structure with a back link, a clear page heading, and a continue action. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Question pages”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10.
- GOV.UK’s radio guidance says to use radios for one choice, checkboxes for multiple choices, group radios in a fieldset with a legend, and avoid pre-selecting an answer. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Radios”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/radios/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Make recovery and validation safe

- GOV.UK says validation errors should explain what went wrong and how to fix it, preserve the user’s entered values, show an error summary at the top, and show field-level errors next to the relevant question. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Help users to Recover from validation errors”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/validation/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10.
- GOV.UK recommends validating when the user continues/submits rather than while they are still typing, and requires server-side validation even when client-side validation is present. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Help users to Recover from validation errors”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/validation/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10.
- GOV.UK’s error-message guidance says not to clear form fields after an error and to provide a concise, actionable message adjacent to the affected field. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Error message”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-message/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Treat uploads as a high-friction, consequential step

- GOV.UK says to ask users to upload a file only when it is critical to delivering the service, and its file-upload component is specifically for helping users select and upload a file. **Verified fact.** Source: [“File upload”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/file-upload/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10.
- **Recommendation:** explain why a credential or identity upload is needed, list accepted types/limits before selection, and offer a non-blocking save/resume state when policy allows. This applies the GOV.UK upload and validation guidance to the platform-specific verification needs documented by italki.

### Provide a reviewable summary

- GOV.UK’s summary-list component is intended to summarize a user’s responses at the end of a form and provides an action area for changing an answer. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Summary list”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/summary-list/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10.
- **Recommendation:** finish onboarding with a “Review profile” screen that shows photo, display name, bio, subjects, experience, availability, verification state, and profile visibility, with a change action for each section.

### Use progress feedback deliberately

- Material Design 3 provides a dedicated progress-indicator component family for communicating progress. **Verified fact.** Source: [“Progress indicators”](https://m3.material.io/components/progress-indicators/overview), Material Design 3, accessed 2026-10-10.
- **Recommendation:** show both section-level status (not started/in progress/complete/needs review) and an overall completion percentage, but do not imply that a percentage alone equals approval or trust. Approval and identity verification should remain separate statuses.

## Recommendations for FindMyTutor

These are implementation recommendations, not verified claims about any external platform.

1. **Use a resumable dashboard onboarding checklist.** Sections: profile photo, display identity, bio, subjects, experience/credentials, availability, verification, and review/publish. Persist each section independently so a teacher can leave and return. This applies the staged italki flow and GOV.UK’s focused question-page pattern ([“How do I apply to become a teacher?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/213716298-How-do-I-apply-to-become-a-teacher), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10; [“Question pages”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10).
2. **Separate “required to publish” from “recommended to improve.”** Required: display name, acceptable photo, bio, at least one subject, experience summary, availability, and verification state. Optional later: intro video, extra subjects, additional credentials, and profile analytics/AI writing help. This is a FindMyTutor product recommendation informed by italki’s required application/verification materials and optional profile-optimization feature ([“Identity Verification Guidelines for Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002630763-Identity-Verification-Guidelines-for-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10; [“Boost Your Profile Feature for italki Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/46097267780249-Boost-Your-Profile-Feature-for-italki-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10).
3. **Collect subjects before credentials, then scope credentials to subjects.** A teacher selecting multiple subjects should see which evidence supports each subject; do not expose original sensitive documents on the public profile. This follows italki’s per-language qualification rule and document-privacy treatment ([“How do I become a Professional Teacher? What are the requirements for certificates?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206352058-How-do-I-become-a-Professional-Teacher-What-are-the-requirements-for-certificates), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10).
4. **Make availability a real editor.** Reuse the existing weekly/exception model in [T04 — Teacher availability](./T04-teacher-availability.md), show timezone explicitly, and keep “temporarily unavailable” as a setting rather than changing/removing the profile photo. The availability and photo distinction is explicitly documented by italki ([“Profile Display Name and Picture Guidelines”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206351988-Profile-Display-Name-and-Picture-Guidelines), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10; [“How to schedule a lesson on italki”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900007728643-How-to-schedule-a-lesson-on-italki), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10).
5. **Use a two-stage verification state.** Model `not_started`, `in_progress`, `verified`, and `needs_action`; explain accepted documents and retention/processor details before upload; keep verification documents private. The state model is a FindMyTutor recommendation; the privacy and identity-verification rationale are grounded in italki’s guidance and GOV.UK’s upload guidance ([“Identity Verification Guidelines for Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002630763-Identity-Verification-Guidelines-for-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10; [“File upload”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/file-upload/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10).
6. **Add a public-profile preview throughout.** Show how the photo crop, bio, subjects, experience, and availability will appear to students. Mark fields as “visible to students” versus “private to FindMyTutor.” This is a recommendation based on italki’s separation between public credential descriptions and private original documents ([“How do I become a Professional Teacher? What are the requirements for certificates?”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206352058-How-do-I-become-a-Professional-Teacher-What-are-the-requirements-for-certificates), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10) and GOV.UK’s reviewable summary pattern ([“Summary list”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/summary-list/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10).
7. **Make profile completion honest.** Calculate completion from publish-critical sections, while showing verification review separately. “90% complete” must not look like “verified.” This recommendation applies Material Design 3’s progress-indicator guidance while preserving the platform-specific distinction between profile setup and identity verification ([“Progress indicators”](https://m3.material.io/components/progress-indicators/overview), Material Design 3, accessed 2026-10-10; [“Identity Verification Guidelines for Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/900002630763-Identity-Verification-Guidelines-for-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10).
8. **Implement accessible, focused forms in the existing architecture.** Put domain validation in `src/features/<feature>/validations/`, service orchestration in `service/`, persistence in `repository/`, and UI in feature `components/`; use the repository’s existing `ServiceResult` and `validateBody` patterns. Preserve entered values and return actionable field errors. The interaction recommendation follows GOV.UK’s question-page, radio, and validation guidance ([“Question pages”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/), [“Radios”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/radios/), and [“Help users to Recover from validation errors”](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/validation/), GOV.UK Design System, accessed 2026-10-10).
9. **Keep media requirements actionable.** For photos, show crop guidance, file types, size, and face/background rules before upload. For a future intro video, provide a short script checklist and technical preview rather than making video a first-session blocker. The concrete photo and video requirements come from italki’s first-party guidance ([“Profile Display Name and Picture Guidelines”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206351988-Profile-Display-Name-and-Picture-Guidelines) and [“Introduction video guidelines and requirements”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/206352028-Introduction-video-guidelines-and-requirements), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10).
10. **Measure completion quality, not only completion rate.** Track section started/saved/completed, verification retries, publish attempts, profile views, and booking starts. Use the metrics to find friction; do not auto-edit teacher content without explicit acceptance. This is a product analytics recommendation informed by italki’s profile views, video plays, booking starts, and booking metrics ([“Boost Your Profile Feature for italki Teachers”](https://support.italki.com/hc/en-us/articles/46097267780249-Boost-Your-Profile-Feature-for-italki-Teachers), italki Support, accessed 2026-10-10).

## Source gaps and limitations

- italki provides unusually detailed public help content for teacher profile media, credentials, verification, languages, video, and booking, but it does not publish a complete current screen-by-screen post-signup onboarding specification or a numeric “profile completion” formula.
- The reviewed Tutor.com page describes the tutor role and flexibility but does not document its post-signup profile editor, photo rules, bio limits, verification flow, or completion meter.
- Preply’s public [“Teach online with Preply”](https://preply.com/en/teach) page reviewed for this report explains tutor earnings, commission, support, and professional development, but did not provide enough directly accessible profile-field or onboarding-step detail to support claims about photo, bio, availability, or verification. **Source reviewed:** [“Teach online with Preply”](https://preply.com/en/teach), Preply, accessed 2026-10-10.
- The official design-system sources provide interaction and accessibility guidance, not tutoring-domain policy. Any FindMyTutor requirements for identity documents, background checks, credential review, retention, or publication must be decided with product, legal, and trust-and-safety owners.
- No source reviewed establishes that a particular photo, bio length, progress meter, or verification sequence universally improves conversion. Treat those as hypotheses to validate with usability testing and funnel data.
